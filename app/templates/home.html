Project Architecture & Pipeline

This application covers the full data science lifecycle from exploratory analysis to deployment:

1. Dataset & Ingestion

Ingests 50,000 verified student profiles across 10 metropolitan hubs with 8-semester SGPA and extracurricular metrics.

2. Exploratory Analysis

Univariate, bivariate, and correlation heatmaps identifying top drivers: CGPA, Aptitude, Internships, and Backlog records.

3. Automated Preprocessing

Median imputation, StandardScaler transformation, one-hot encoding for cities/streams, and ordinal encoding for tier rankings.

4. Multi-Model Suite

Trained Logistic Regression, ID3 Decision Trees, Random Forests, Regularized Regressions, Batch Gradient Descent, K-Means, and DBSCAN.
